THE METHOD / USE IT EVERY TIME

Think first.
Change one thing.

Troubleshooting means finding the cause of a problem by checking possible explanations, one step at a time.

01

Identify

What is not working? When did it start? Read the exact error message.

02

Check

Look at power, connections, settings, and what recently changed.

03

Try

Save your work. Make one small, appropriate change.

04

Test

Repeat the original task. If it still fails, record the result and try the next step.

Protect the work.
Respect shared devices.
